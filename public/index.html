Set up Gmail once

Google needs you to make a free sign-in key for PackTrack. Do each step, tap Open, then come back. It's easiest on a computer.

  1. Sign in with your Gmail, name it PackTrack, tap Create.

    Open ↗

  2. Check PackTrack is the selected project at the top, then tap Enable.

    Open ↗

  3. Tap Get started. App name PackTrack, pick your email, Audience External, your email again, agree, Create.

    Open ↗

  4. Under Test users tap Add users, type your Gmail address, Save.

    Open ↗

  5. Type Web application. Then add these two. Tap to copy, then paste each:

    Tap Create and copy the Client ID it shows.

    Open ↗

  6. Google then says “Google hasn't verified this app”. That's normal for your own app: tap Continue.

Gmail is read-only and your mail never leaves your phone. Google signs PackTrack out after an hour; tap Check Gmail now to sign back in.
